# Shipping policy

> Synthetic document for the Switchboard demo. It is not a real marketplace policy.

Orders are shipped from the seller's warehouse after payment is approved.
Customers see an estimated delivery date on the order page. The estimate is
based on the destination region, the seller's handling time, and the carrier's
service level.

## Handling time

Sellers must hand the parcel to the carrier within two business days after
payment approval. Orders paid by boleto wait for bank confirmation before the
handling clock starts. A seller who misses the handling window may receive a
late shipment warning.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes three to seven business days in the southeast region
and five to twelve business days elsewhere. Remote areas can take up to twenty
business days. Express delivery is available for selected sellers and takes one
to three business days. These ranges are estimates, not guarantees.

## Carriers and tracking

Each parcel has a tracking code. The order page shows the main tracking events:
handed to carrier, in transit, out for delivery, and delivered. A tracking
event can lag behind the physical parcel by up to 24 hours. Customers should
contact the carrier first when a parcel is marked as delivered but not found.

## Delays

Weather, strikes, and customs checks can delay a parcel. When the estimated
delivery date passes, the order page shows the parcel as late. A late parcel
may qualify for the compensation described in the late delivery policy. The
customer can request a cancellation when the parcel has not arrived 21 days
after the estimated date.

## Address changes

The delivery address can be changed until the seller hands the parcel to the
carrier. After that point the carrier rules apply and a change may not be
possible. This document is synthetic and exists only for the Switchboard demo.
