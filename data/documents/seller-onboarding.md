# Seller onboarding

> Synthetic document for the Switchboard demo. It is not a real marketplace policy.

Any registered company with a valid tax id can apply to sell on the
marketplace. The application is reviewed before the first product is published.

## Creating an account

The applicant creates a seller account with an email address and confirms it.
The next step asks for the company legal name, tax id, and a contact phone. A
person can hold more than one seller account only with written approval.

## Required documents

The seller uploads a company registration document, a proof of address, and
bank account details in the company name. The bank account is used for payouts
and must match the tax id. Documents are checked within three business days.

## Catalogue and policies

Before publishing, the seller accepts the product listing rules, the returns
policy, and the shipping policy. Listings need a clear title, at least one
photo, the product dimensions, and the warranty terms. Prohibited items cannot
be listed.

## Commission and payouts

The marketplace commission is twelve percent of the item value. The commission
is deducted before the payout. Payouts run every two weeks for delivered orders
whose return window has closed. A new seller may see a longer hold period until
the first ten orders are delivered.

## Getting help

New sellers can open a support ticket from the seller dashboard. The onboarding
team answers within two business days. This document is synthetic and exists
only to exercise retrieval in the Switchboard demo.
