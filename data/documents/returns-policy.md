# Returns policy

> Synthetic document for the Switchboard demo. It is not a real marketplace policy.

Customers may return most items for any reason within the standard return
window. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all
accessories. The return request is opened in the order page and the customer
prints a prepaid label when the item qualifies.

## Standard return window

The standard return window is 30 calendar days from the delivery date. A
request opened on the last day is accepted. Orders marked as delivered after
the estimated date still count from the actual delivery date.

## Electronics

Electronics have a shorter window. The return window for electronics is 15
calendar days from delivery. Unopened electronics can be returned for any
reason. Opened electronics can be returned only when they are defective, and
the customer must describe the defect in the return request. Accessories such
as chargers and cables follow the same 15 day window.

## Refunds

After the returned item arrives at the warehouse it is inspected within two
business days. A refund is issued to the original payment method within five
business days after inspection. Shipping fees are refunded when the return is
caused by a defect or a wrong item. The original delivery time is not refunded
for a change of mind.

## Exceptions

Personalized items, perishable goods, and digital codes cannot be returned
unless they arrive damaged or defective. Damaged items must be reported within
48 hours of delivery with a photo. Items returned without a return request may
be refused at the warehouse.

## Questions

Customers can ask about a specific order in the order page. The support team
answers within one business day. This document is synthetic and exists only to
exercise retrieval and citation behavior in the Switchboard demo.
