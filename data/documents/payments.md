# Payments

> Synthetic document for the Switchboard demo. It is not a real marketplace policy.

The marketplace accepts credit cards, boleto, and Pix. The available methods
depend on the seller and on the order value. All prices are shown in Brazilian
reais and include applicable taxes.

## Credit cards

Credit card payments are approved immediately in most cases. An issuer may ask
for additional verification, which can take up to one business day. Cards from
the major networks are accepted. The card is charged once, at checkout, and the
customer never sends card details to the seller.

## Installments

Orders above 50 reais can be split into up to 12 installments. The number of
interest-free installments depends on the seller. The checkout shows the
monthly amount and the total before the customer confirms. Installments appear
on the card statement as separate monthly charges.

## Boleto and Pix

A boleto is a bank slip that must be paid before its due date, usually the next
business day. Pix is immediate and is confirmed in seconds. Orders paid by
boleto or Pix are released to the seller after the payment is confirmed. An
unpaid boleto cancels the order automatically after the due date.

## Refunds and chargebacks

Refunds go to the original payment method. Card refunds appear in one or two
statements, depending on the issuer. Pix refunds are returned to the account
that made the payment. A chargeback opens a dispute; the seller is asked for
evidence and the amount is held until the dispute is resolved.

## Security

The payment page uses the marketplace checkout and never asks for a card
password by email or chat. Suspicious messages should be reported. This
document is synthetic and exists only for the Switchboard demo.
