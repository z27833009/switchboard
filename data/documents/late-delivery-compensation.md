# Late delivery compensation policy

> Synthetic document for the Switchboard demo. It is not a real marketplace policy.

A delivery is late when the parcel reaches the customer after the estimated
delivery date shown on the order page. The delivery date is the timestamp
recorded by the carrier when the customer receives the parcel.

## Eligibility

A delivery is eligible for late-delivery compensation when it arrives at least
three calendar days after the estimated delivery date. The order must not be
canceled, and the customer account must be in good standing. The request must
be opened within 60 calendar days of the delivery. Orders with an address
correction requested by the customer are not eligible for the days lost while
the address was being corrected.

## Compensation

Eligible customers receive a voucher equal to ten percent of the item value,
with a minimum of five reais and a maximum of fifty reais. The voucher is
credited to the customer account within three business days after approval and
expires in 90 days. A voucher cannot be exchanged for cash and can be combined
with one other promotion.

## How to request

The customer opens the order page and chooses "Request late delivery
compensation". The request form asks for the order id and a short description.
The team checks the carrier record and answers within two business days. An
approved request creates one voucher per order, even when the order has several
items.

## Exclusions

A delivery is not eligible when the estimated date was already extended before
the parcel left the seller, when the customer refused the parcel, or when the
delay was caused by an incorrect address supplied by the customer. Force
majeure events listed by the carrier are reviewed case by case.
